### 1. Commercial model

#### Proposed supply and service model

The proposed model combines product purchases with separately contracted services, while clinical and pharmacy providers retain all clinical decisions and responsibilities.

| Category | Customer or contracting party | What is provided | Revenue type | Frequency | Status | Provider responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patient supply purchases | Patients or caregivers | Proposed pharmacist-selected non-drug supplies, or metabolic-health starter equipment and usage-driven consumables; medication excluded | Product revenue | Occasional equipment purchases; recurring consumable purchases as needed | Product retail is current; these programs are in development and unavailable | Supply selection within clinical care, prescribing, treatment and all other clinical decisions |
| Clinic-wide procurement | Clinics | Proposed equipment and consumable procurement for metabolic-health programs | Product revenue | Occasional equipment purchases; recurring replenishment as needed | In development; unavailable | Clinical suitability, selection and use of supplies, and patient care |
| Contracted kitting and fulfilment | Clinical or pharmacy providers | Proposed assembly and fulfilment of non-drug supply kits | Service fee | Occasional or recurring, as a future contract would specify | In development; unavailable | Clinical requirements, suitability of kit contents and patient care |
| Contracted non-clinical workflow and reporting support | Clinical or pharmacy providers | Proposed administrative workflow support; any reporting scope would require definition | Service fee | Occasional or recurring, as a future contract would specify | Workflow support is in development and unavailable; reporting is not established | All clinical decisions, interpretation and patient care |

This table describes a proposed model and establishes no contract or integration between brands or with providers.
