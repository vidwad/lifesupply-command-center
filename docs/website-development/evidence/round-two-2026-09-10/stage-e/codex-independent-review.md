## 1. Substantive findings, ranked by seriousness

1. **The commercial model invents commercial terms and service scope.**  
   In `metabolic.ts`:
   > “A service fee, separate from the products themselves.”  
   > “A service fee.”  
   > “The clinic or pharmacy, under a written scope agreed in advance.”  
   > “Assembly of non-drug supply kits, direct shipment, replenishment administration, and exception handling.”

   **Rule:** No unverified claims or invented contracts. The facts establish kitting, fulfilment and contracted non-clinical workflow support in development, but not fee structures, contracting procedures, direct shipment, replenishment administration or exception handling. Calling this a “proposed model” does not establish those details.

   **Correction:** “In development: non-drug supply kitting and fulfilment, and contracted non-clinical workflow support. Clinical and pharmacy providers retain all clinical decisions.” Remove unsupported revenue, contracting and service-detail entries.

2. **Clinic procurement is presented as an established offering with an unsupported purchasing assurance.**  
   In `metabolic.ts`, the clinic-wide procurement row says:
   > “Operates today. No project or agreement is required.”

   **Rule:** Programs in development must not appear available; no unverified commercial terms. Ordinary store purchasing and the clinic-procurement component of the developing metabolic-health service need clearer separation. Neither the current procurement offering nor the assurance about agreements is established.

   **Correction:** “Clinic procurement within the metabolic-health supply service is in development and is not currently offered.” Describe existing store commerce separately using the verified brand facts.

3. **The contact copy promises a future commercial process.**  
   In `contact.ts`:
   > “If it does, scope, responsibilities, and any commercial terms are defined in writing after that, before anything runs.”

   In `metabolic.ts`:
   > “Any recurring arrangement would be proposed separately and would depend on terms agreed in writing.”

   **Rule:** No invented contractual process; future statements must not read as commitments. The first passage promises what will happen after a suitable inquiry. The second introduces an unverified recurring-arrangement process.

   **Correction:** “Inquiries may concern current operations or programs in development. Programs in development are not currently offered.” Delete the recurring-arrangement sentence.

4. **Investor copy promises access to material whose availability is unverified.**  
   In `investors.ts`:
   > “Where a document is restricted, the figure shown on this page is the extent of what is published. Everything else is available on request through investor relations.”

   **Rule:** No unverified claims or commitments; no publishing or source-handling commentary. Neither document restrictions nor access to “everything else” is established. The financing presentation’s existence and date do not establish its availability.

   **Correction:** “A financing presentation dated August 25, 2026 exists. Investor inquiries: invest@lifesupply.com.”

5. **Retained investor copy asserts unverified dependencies and their current status.**  
   In `investors.ts`:
   > “They depend on approvals, financing, regulatory requirements, and transaction terms that have not been obtained”

   Also:
   > “LifeSupply undertakes no obligation to update them except as required.”

   **Rule:** Only verified facts. The supplied facts do not establish particular approval or financing dependencies, whether any have been obtained, or an adopted disclosure policy. “Regulatory requirements” and “market conditions” also are not things that can be “obtained.”

   **Correction:** “The supply programs are in development. Pharmacy-related operations and regulated care infrastructure are under evaluation and are not currently offered, licensed or operating. Development and evaluation do not establish a commitment to proceed.”

6. **The homepage contains an unsupported founding-investor claim.**  
   In `home.ts`:
   > “a founding investor base of medical practitioners and specialists alongside investment bankers and capital-market professionals”

   **Rule:** No claim beyond the verified facts. This remains in the replacement sentence but is not established.

   **Correction:** “LifeSupply has more than 25 years of operations. Its operating brands conduct commerce in Canada and the United States and provide clinic planning, design, construction, fit-out and equipment services for projects in British Columbia.”

7. **The accessibility paragraph asserts unverified testing and review status and exposes release mechanics.**  
   In `policies.ts`:
   > “The site has not been audited by an external accessibility reviewer, and no such review is booked.”

   > “Automated checks run against every public route on each release: one main heading per page, a heading order that never skips a level, alternative text on every image, an accessible name on every link, no horizontal scrolling at 390 pixels, and visible keyboard focus.”

   **Rule:** No unverified claims; no publishing mechanics. Neither review status nor the testing process is among the verified facts. This finding concerns the assertions, not an assessment of the site’s layout or accessibility.

   **Correction:** Remove these assertions. A factual contact alternative is: “Website accessibility inquiries: info@lifesupply.com.”

8. **The pathway comparison adds unsupported programme-design rules.**  
   In `metabolic.ts`:
   > “They overlap on purpose: a program can draw on more than one, and none of them excludes another.”

   > “no configuration has been published, and starter equipment is chosen once rather than replenished on a cycle.”

   Also:
   > “Starter equipment once; consumables as they are used.”

   **Rule:** Only verified facts; no publishing commentary. Eight configurable pathways do not establish deliberate overlap, combinations, publication status or fixed equipment-purchase frequency.

   **Correction:** “Eight configurable metabolic-health supply pathways are in development and are not currently available for purchase.” Remove the unsupported configuration and frequency rules.

9. **The account wording can imply a common account.**  
   In `businesses.ts`:
   > “Every customer uses the same store, the same account, and the same checkout.”

   **Rule:** No implication of shared accounts or systems. Even within the LifeSupply.ca entry, “every customer” using “the same account” is misleading.

   **Correction:** “LifeSupply.ca has its own site, accounts, currency, prices and support.”

10. **The contact guide gives an unjustified assurance about information and uses second person.**  
    In `contact.ts`:
    > “None of them commits you to anything, and none of them is personal or clinical information.”

    > “Your organization and your role in it.”

    **Rule:** No unverified assurances; plain third person. Organisation and role information can identify a person, and an open description of needs can contain clinical information. The categorical assurance conflicts with the caution below it.

    **Correction:** “Initial inquiries can include the organisation, business role, operating region and a broad description of the business need. Messages should exclude health information, prescriptions, and account or payment details.” Delete the commitment assurance and recast the remaining prompts in third person.

11. **Several visible factual details exceed the supplied evidence.**
    - `contact.ts`: **“1-855-755-5433”** is not a verified inquiry destination. Remove it; use **info@lifesupply.com**.
    - `contact.ts`: **“6911 King George Highway, Surrey, British Columbia V3W 5A1”** is verified as the corporate office, but its placement under Wellmart assigns it to that entity without evidence. Label it explicitly as the parent’s corporate office.
    - `investors.ts`: **“Four regulated options under evaluation”** introduces an unverified count. Replace with **“Pharmacy-related operations and regulated care infrastructure under evaluation.”**
    - `businesses.ts`: **“sells medical, health, wellness, and related categories”** and **“a broad general catalogue”** describe Balkowitsch’s assortment beyond the supplied facts. Replace with **“Balkowitsch Worldwide is the group’s United States operating brand, with its own site, accounts, USD currency, prices and support.”**

12. **The currency explanation exposes source-handling commentary.**  
    In `investors.ts`:
    > “Currency: the annual-report narrative does not state one, so none is given here.”

    **Rule:** No source-handling or drafting commentary. This correctly avoids inventing a currency, but explains the editorial decision to the public.

    **Correction:** “Reporting currency: unspecified.”

## 2. Weaker findings

- **“This is how the four of them are proposed to fit together”** has an unclear antecedent: the preceding sentence names only products and services.
- **“under the same kind of written scope”** is vague and depends on another table row for meaning.
- **“it keeps its identity”** adds little after the specific explanation of separate brand operations.
- **“the five strands and the status of each, and the business case for the two in development”** is cumbersome. “Growth areas, their status and the rationale for the programs in development” is clearer.
- **“the one the operating stores name”** makes the corporate-structure explanation unnecessarily indirect. State the verified Wellmart relationship directly.

- What distinguishes Wellmart Medical from LifeSupply.ca, including its products and intended buyers.
- Which legal entity would contract for clinic projects or any future supply service.
- Where the developing pharmacy and metabolic-health programs would operate.
- What the eight pathways contain; the underlying pathway entries are not shown here.
- Whether or when the developing programs will launch, and their eventual scope or commercial terms.
- Whether the financing presentation can be obtained, and whether any financing is currently being sought.
- What reporting currency underlies the 2025 financial figures.

Several of these gaps require further verified information; copy changes alone cannot resolve them.
